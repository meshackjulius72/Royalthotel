Dining

Eat and drink in the hotel.

Caspian Restaurant serves Arabian, Asian and European dishes made with fresh local ingredients, and the hotel also lists an Indian restaurant.

A coffee shop is open 24 hours. The bar serves cocktails and draft beer. Room service is available, and buffet breakfast is offered for a fee.

Good to know: the hotel has bars and nightlife on site, and some guests report music late at night. If you are a light sleeper, tell the hotel when you book.
